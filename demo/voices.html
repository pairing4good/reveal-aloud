Not good enough? Try Kokoro.

An open, free text-to-speech model that runs entirely in this browser tab — no account, no server, no per-use cost. It sounds far less robotic than the system voices above. The cost is a one-time download (tens of MB) and a short pause before each sentence while it generates the audio.

Already downloaded good voices on your Mac?

Route through the macOS say command instead, and use exactly what you already have in System Settings — including a Siri voice, which cannot reach a browser any other way. This needs one small helper running on your own machine.
